# WUTT AI Stylist

## Your personal AI fashion assistant

WUTT helps users discover better outfits through AI-powered styling,
wardrobe management, and personalized fashion recommendations.

Built with an AI-assisted development workflow.

---

# What WUTT does

## AI-powered fashion experience

- Personal wardrobe management
- AI stylist conversation
- Outfit recommendations
- Occasion-based styling suggestions
- Personalized fashion guidance

---

# User Experience Flow

1. Add wardrobe items

2. Tell WUTT your occasion or style goal

3. Chat with AI stylist

4. Receive personalized outfit suggestions
